# OneLake (sample)
id: fabric-m01-onelake
track: fabric
verified: pending

## What we're learning
OneLake is the single logical data lake that comes with every Fabric tenant. All Fabric items store their data in it.

## Benefit
One copy of the data can serve Spark, SQL and Power BI without copying it between services.

## Remember this
OneLake is one lake per tenant, shared by every Fabric workload.
